A featherweight daily runner with a responsive foam midsole and a sock-like knit upper. Built for the commute, the 10k, and everything between.

Description
The Nova Runner pairs a 34mm stack of responsive foam with a rocker geometry that rolls you through each stride. The engineered knit upper breathes where you run hot and holds where you need lockdown, and the bone midsole gives it a clean, off-track look.
Features
  • Responsive dual-density foam midsole
  • Engineered knit upper with recycled yarns
  • Rubber outsole with multi-directional tread
  • Padded heel collar and pull tab
  • Weight: 8.6 oz (men's size 9)
Materials & care
Upper: 70% recycled polyester knit, 30% TPU overlays. Midsole: EVA foam. Outsole: carbon rubber. Spot clean with a soft brush and mild soap; air dry away from direct heat.
